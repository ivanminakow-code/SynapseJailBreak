How To Install with Scarlet

Follow these steps to install SynapseJB on your iPhone or iPad

1

Open Safari and visit Scarlet

Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad and go to the official Scarlet website. Tap the Install button and choose Direct Install.

2

Install the Scarlet app

When prompted, tap Install and wait for the Scarlet icon to appear on your home screen. The installation may take up to a minute.

3

Trust the developer profile

Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Find the enterprise developer profile and tap Trust. Confirm the action.

4

Download SynapseJB.ipa

Tap the Get SynapseJB button on this page to download the .ipa file to your device. It will be saved in your Files app.

5

Import and install in Scarlet

Open Scarlet, tap the import button, and select SynapseJB.ipa from your Files. Tap install and wait for the process to complete.

6

Trust and launch

After installation, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management again and trust the new profile. Then open SynapseJB from your home screen.

⚠️ Note: Scarlet uses enterprise certificates that Apple may revoke at any time. If the app stops working, you may need to reinstall it or wait for a new certificate.
